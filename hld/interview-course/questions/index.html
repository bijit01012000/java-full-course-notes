P1 — High value

12 questions
13Handle Hot KeysA hot key saturates one node while the rest of the fleet is idle, so spread reads with a local cache and N key copies, spread writes with split counters, and protect the origin with coalescing, negative caching, and a per-key limit.→14Absorb Traffic SpikesA spike is about ramp speed, not average load: have capacity ready with headroom and pre-warming, cap demand with rate limits and admission control, buffer only in bounded queues, and shed or degrade so the critical flow survives.→15Fan Out UpdatesFan-out is a decision about when to do the distribution work: push at publish time for ordinary accounts, pull at read time for high-follower accounts, and merge both into a cursor-paginated feed with idempotent writes.→16Upload and Serve Large FilesAuthorize the transfer with a short-lived presigned URL, upload directly to object storage in resumable parts, verify checksums before the file becomes visible, process asynchronously, and serve through a CDN with range support.→17WebSockets vs SSE vs PollingPick the transport from direction and freshness, then solve routing separately: polling for rare updates, SSE for one-way streams, WebSockets for frequent two-way traffic, with pub/sub or a connection registry to find the right server and sequence numbers to recover what was missed.→18Distributed Transactions / SAGAKeep the work in one local transaction if you can; use 2PC only for short internal writes; otherwise run a saga of local transactions with idempotent compensations, an outbox for reliable events, and explicit recovery for stuck workflows.→19Run Across Multiple RegionsRoute users to the nearest healthy region, keep one writer per data partition, replicate asynchronously with a measured RPO, and fail over only with a quorum, fencing, and a rehearsed runbook.→20Consistent HashingPlace servers and keys on the same fixed hash ring, give each server many virtual nodes for balance, and move only the arc owned by a joining or leaving node.→21API Pagination and VersioningUse signed keyset cursors over a stable sort key, keep responses additive within a major version, and deprecate old versions with telemetry and a published timeline.→22Search and TypeaheadKeep the transactional database as the source of truth, build a sharded inverted index for ranked search, and serve typeahead from cached prefix top-k with debouncing and a separate latency budget.→23JWT vs Server-side SessionsDefault to server-side sessions for a first-party app, use short-lived JWTs when many services must verify identity locally, and keep refresh tokens opaque, rotated, and revocable.→24TLS and mTLSTerminate TLS at the edge with automated certificates, re-encrypt inside the network, and use mTLS with attested, short-lived workload identities for service-to-service authentication.→

P2 — Role-specific

8 questions
25Design ObservabilityInstrument every service with structured logs, low-cardinality metrics, and sampled traces that share a trace ID, then page on SLO burn for user-visible symptoms while keeping logs and traces one click from the alert.→26Circuit Breaker and BulkheadWrap each remote dependency in a per-dependency state machine that trips on failure or slow-call rate, rejects fast with a safe fallback, probes recovery in half-open, and caps concurrency with a bulkhead and retry budget.→27Serverless ArchitectureServerless is managed event-driven compute plus managed services: the provider handles provisioning and patching, while idempotency, concurrency limits, cold starts, peak-cost economics, and lock-in stay design problems.→28Event-Driven ArchitectureProducers publish immutable past-tense facts to a partitioned broker, and consumers subscribe and react idempotently, trading immediate consistency and simple debugging for temporal decoupling, buffering, and independent evolution.→29Long-Running Tasks and SchedulingAccept the work synchronously into a durable job record, return 202 with a job ID, and let leased workers plus a scheduler execute it idempotently with retries, progress, and dead-lettering.→30Location-Based SearchMap latitude and longitude to hierarchical cells, query the cell plus its neighbours, then filter by exact distance; keep live positions in a TTL cache and places in a spatial store.→31Generate Unique IDsGenerate IDs locally from a timestamp, worker, and sequence layout, lease worker identities, and keep a separate random public identifier when enumeration matters.→32Counting at ScaleSplit hot counters into shards or aggregate a durable event stream, keep the display path cached and approximate, and reserve exact counts for money.→
