    A2Z DSA Sheet - Overview

    1 sections · interview-oriented · both themes

    The A2Z DSA Sheet is a from-scratch roadmap that covers every major data structure and algorithm in one ordered track, pairing each problem with a hint, a short approach, a Java solution and its complexity. It is divided into 17 topics that build on one another, moving from sorting and arrays through linked lists, trees, graphs and dynamic programming. The pages below are clean, revision-ready notes extracted from the sheet topic by topic. Work through them in order, or jump straight to a topic for a quick refresher before an interview.
